SportSense AI Pro

Athletes record five fitness tests on a phone or webcam. The app measures them and suggests the sports they suit best.

  • Python backend measures joint angles, reps and jump height from video
  • Face check stops anyone else taking the test
  • ML model ranks suitability for six sports

Personal project, 2026

CloudGuard Digital Legacy Vault

A vault for important documents. If the owner stops checking in, a verified process hands them to the people they chose.

  • Four roles: owner, nominee, recipient and reviewer
  • JWT and bcrypt login with 22 Row Level Security policies
  • Deadline scheduler with grace periods and email alerts
  • Face check-in and PDF checks to support human reviewers
